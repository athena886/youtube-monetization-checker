Why YPPCheck exists

Useful context without a complicated dashboard

Creators often have to jump between YouTube Studio, spreadsheets, policy pages, and generic calculators to answer a simple question: what do these channel numbers actually mean? YPPCheck brings the most useful public signals into focused, plain-English tools. There is no account to create and no request for access to a private channel. You can begin with a public URL or a monthly-view estimate and get an immediate result.

The eligibility tool compares public channel activity with the two main YouTube Partner Program milestones. It shows subscriber progress, recent public uploads, modeled long-form watch time, and estimated Shorts activity. The report also explains where public information ends. YouTube does not expose a channel’s enrollment, policy standing, valid Studio watch hours, or actual revenue through its public API, so those fields are always presented as estimates rather than facts.

The revenue tool approaches a different planning question. It combines monthly views with RPM—the amount a creator earns per 1,000 total views after YouTube’s share. Presets help you explore common content categories, while the adjustable control lets you replace a preset with a more relevant scenario. If you already have historical RPM inside YouTube Studio, that private number will normally be a better planning input.

How to use the tools

Move from curiosity to a practical next step

Start with the channel report when you want to understand an existing creator, competitor, prospect, or brand partner. Paste a handle URL, permanent channel ID, or legacy custom address. Review the numerical milestones first, then read the estimate notes before treating the result as a business decision. A green status means the public model clears the main numerical threshold; it does not prove approval or current ad revenue.

Use the earnings model when planning a new upload schedule, setting a growth target, or comparing content categories. Try several combinations instead of relying on one headline number. Geography, seasonality, video length, advertiser demand, ad suitability, audience behavior, Shorts usage, and other revenue sources can all change the outcome. Sponsorships, memberships, affiliate income, products, taxes, and production costs are outside the calculator.

Results from the channel tool are cached for 24 hours. This keeps repeat checks fast, reduces unnecessary requests to YouTube, and makes the service more reliable during traffic spikes. Public statistics may still be rounded or delayed, and private YouTube Studio metrics remain the authoritative source for a channel owner.

Quick answers

What to know before using YPPCheck

Are the tools free?

Yes. Both tools are free to use, require no YPPCheck account, and do not require access to a YouTube channel.

How accurate are the results?

Public counts come from YouTube data. Watch time, Shorts classification, eligibility status, RPM, and revenue are modeled estimates because YouTube does not publish the necessary private Studio metrics.

How often is channel information refreshed?

A completed channel report is cached for 24 hours. A later check refreshes the report using the public information available at that time.

Does YPPCheck confirm that a channel earns ad revenue?

No. Only the channel owner can confirm enrollment and revenue inside YouTube Studio. YPPCheck provides an evidence-based estimate from public signals.
