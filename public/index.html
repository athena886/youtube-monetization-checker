Quick answers

Before you check a channel

Can you tell whether a YouTube channel is definitely monetized?

No public API reveals definitive YPP enrollment. YPPCheck estimates likely eligibility from public channel data and recent-video activity; only the channel owner can confirm status in YouTube Studio.

Is the checker free?

Yes. The monetization checker and earnings calculator are free and require no account.

Does seeing ads mean a channel is monetized?

No. YouTube may place ads on content from channels outside YPP, and rights holders can monetize claimed content.

What URLs are supported?

You can use a current handle URL, a permanent channel-ID URL, or a legacy custom channel URL.
